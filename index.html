Meet the Artist

Hello, I'm Ananya — your beauty storyteller.

For over 5 years, I've had the privilege of being part of life's most beautiful moments — from intimate engagements to grand destination weddings. My philosophy is simple: makeup should never mask who you are, it should reveal the most radiant version of you.

Every look is tailored to your skin, your features and your story. I work exclusively with premium, skin-loving brands like Charlotte Tilbury, MAC, Huda Beauty and Dior, and follow strict hygiene protocols so you can simply relax and glow.
